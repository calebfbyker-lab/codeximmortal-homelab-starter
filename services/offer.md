# Services

CodexImmortal offers help with small-network builds. Scope only; pricing to be agreed per engagement.

## Possible engagements
1. **Setup help:** firewall, switch and access point installation and documentation.
2. **Review:** a read-only check of an existing home or small-office network against the hardening checklist.
3. **Monitoring setup:** deploying a read-only reachability or dashboard tool once verified.

## What is not offered
- Guarantees of security or compliance.
- Testing of networks you do not own or are not authorized to test.
