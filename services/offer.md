# Services

CodexImmortal offers hands-on help with small-network builds. This page describes scope. Pricing is agreed per engagement, and no prices are published yet. Results depend on your equipment and environment, and no outcome is guaranteed.

## Engagements

### 1. Setup help

Installation and documentation of a firewall, switch and access point.

- **Typical deliverables:** a working configuration based on the reference layout, a current network diagram, a record of what was changed and when, and your hardening checklist filled in for the items that were checked.
- **You provide:** the equipment, ISP details and physical or remote access agreed in advance.

### 2. Network review

A read-only check of an existing home or small-office network against the hardening checklist.

- **Typical deliverables:** a written summary of what was checked, findings ranked by importance, and recommended fixes.
- **You provide:** written authorization for the networks and devices in scope.
- **Not included:** changes to your network unless you separately agree to them.

### 3. Monitoring setup

Read-only reachability monitoring of your own devices once the tooling has been verified in your environment.

- **Typical deliverables:** a configured reachability check, a sanitized device inventory, and instructions for running and reading the results.
- **Status:** the underlying tool is unit-tested but not yet run against a production network. We will say so in the scope before starting.

## What is not offered

- Guarantees of security or compliance.
- Penetration testing, or testing of networks you do not own or are not authorized to test.
- Emergency incident response.
