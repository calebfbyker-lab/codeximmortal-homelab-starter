# Engagement process

How a paid engagement works, from first contact to handoff. Details are agreed per engagement, and this page describes the intended flow.

## 1. Intake

Share what you have and what you want: the equipment, your ISP setup, the number of devices, and the outcome you are after. Do not send passwords, firewall exports or VPN configs at this stage.

## 2. Written scope and authorization

Before any work, we agree in writing:

- Which networks and devices are in scope.
- What will and will not be changed.
- That you own the systems or are authorized to approve work on them.
- The price and payment terms.

We do not test or change anything outside the agreed scope.

## 3. Access

- Use temporary, limited accounts where possible.
- Do not send credentials by email or chat. Agree a safer method first.
- You revoke or rotate access when the engagement ends.

## 4. Work

- **Setup help:** done step by step with a record of every change.
- **Network review:** read-only. Findings are collected but nothing is changed.
- **Monitoring setup:** read-only tooling restricted to your private addresses.

## 5. Handoff

You receive the agreed deliverables, such as a network diagram, a change log, a completed checklist or a written review. Sensitive details are kept out of anything published.

## 6. After the engagement

Rotate any credentials that were shared. Keep your configuration backups and records private, and never commit them to a public repository.

## Boundaries

- No guarantees of security or compliance.
- No work on networks you do not own or are not authorized to approve.
- No emergency incident response.
